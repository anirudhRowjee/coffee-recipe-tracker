How this works

  1. Add a coffee and the bag you bought.
  2. Brew it. Start brewing opens a timer that walks you through each step.
  3. Rate it, and say what you'd try next time.
  4. Try one change: grind, temperature, coffee, water or the steps. It's saved as a new version.
  5. Repeat until it's the cup you want.
