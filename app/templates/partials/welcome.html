Welcome to the app

Three things to set up, and you're ready to brew:

  1. Add your coffee and its bag. On Manage, add the coffee, then the bag you bought.
  2. Add a brewer and a grinder. Also on Manage. Using pre-ground? Add a grinder called "No Grinder".
  3. Add a first recipe. Back on Brew, pick your bag, brewer and grinder, then save the recipe you'd normally start with.
