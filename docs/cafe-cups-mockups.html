Café cups: log and share coffee you drink out

A café cup is separate from your recipes. Nothing here is a version, a bag or a dial-in step: it's a diary of what you drank, where, how good it was, and a photo, with a card you can post. It lives with your coffees, takes about 20 seconds to log, and only the café, the kind of drink and a star rating are needed.

1 · Where it lives

A fourth tab, Cafés, between Coffees and Settings, with Mine | Friends at the top. Coffees stays as it is. On Brew there's also a quiet "☕ Had one out? Log a café cup" link at the bottom.

3 · The share card

Like Share as image for recipes: drawn in the browser, then the phone's share sheet (Instagram, WhatsApp, anything) or a download. The photo takes the top, the words sit on a plain card below it (never on the photo, so they stay readable), and the border takes the photo's colours. You choose what goes on it: rating, tasting note, the coffee, price. Your handle goes in the corner.

5 · Friends: café cups people chose to share

The second switch on Cafés. Only people signed in to the app see it (past the site password and their own account); there's no public link. It shows cups whose owner turned on "Show in the friends' feed", newest first. It's look-only: nobody can change someone else's cup, and turning the switch off takes a cup out of the feed at once. No likes, comments or follows for now.

To decide

  1. Where it lives: a fourth tab, Cafés (decided).
  2. Your handle: remembered on your account and editable on each cup (as drawn), or typed fresh each time?
  3. Private by default: a café cup is yours unless you turn on "Show in the friends' feed" (off by default). The share card shows only what you switch on; price is off by default.
  4. The one rule change: "nobody sees anyone else's things" gets one written, tested exception: café cups their owner put in the feed are readable, never changeable, by other signed-in people. Everything else, and every edit and delete, stays locked to its owner.
  5. "Bought the beans?" only fills in the Add coffee form (from your cup or a friend's). No lasting link between the coffee and the café cup for now.
  6. Later, not now: likes, comments and follows; a café map; café stats on Settings; a lasting "first had at" link.
