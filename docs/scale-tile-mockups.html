Brewing with the scale: the target first

At the moment the weight is the big number and the target is a small grey line under it ("g → 96 g"), so the one number only the app knows is the hardest to read. Each option below shows the same moment of a brew three ways: pouring the second pour (0:45, to 96 g, at 58.3 g), at the target, and 6 g past it. The clock tile stays as it is.

A · "Pour to", big Recommended

The tiles swap roles: the target is the big number, and what the scale reads goes underneath at about twice today's size. It's the same tile as without a scale ("Pour to"), so brewing with and without a scale looks alike. The bar still fills as you pour.

C · A full-width pour bar

The weight leaves the tiles altogether: a thick bar across the whole width fills as you pour, with the target big at its end and the reading inside the fill. The step card goes under it. A glance tells you how far there is to go without reading any number; the right-hand tile goes back to "Step ends in".

To decide

  • A is the smallest change and fixes exactly what's wrong: the target is the big number, and the reading is still there, larger than today's grey line.
  • B is the most "do this now", but a number that counts down to 0 is one more thing to learn, and the target itself is smaller than in A.
  • C reads best from across the room, and costs the most height, which matters on a phone where the step card and animation already compete for space.
  • They mix: A's tile with C's thicker bar is an easy addition.
