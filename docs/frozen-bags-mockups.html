Frozen portions, simplified

Split a bag into portions. Each portion gets a short code at once. Write the code on the bag, or print a PDF of labels at Printo with everything on them. Anyone can look a code up on one page. Taking one out is one tap.

❄ Freeze in portions→Codes, and a PDF of labels→Take it out or Gave it away

1 · Freeze, then label

Two screens. A portion is an ordinary bag with a code, so brewing, grams left and history don't change. No label sheet to buy in advance, nothing to scan or pair: the code exists the moment you tap Freeze.

2 · The PDF: an A4 sheet for Printo

Ten labels per A4 sheet (2 × 5, about 99 × 57 mm each), with dashed lines to cut along with scissors, so any vinyl sheet works and nothing has to line up. Each label has the QR code, the code, the coffee, its dates and weight, and the best recipe's numbers. Three portions print three labels; the rest of the sheet is left blank. Every coffee's page has "⬇ Labels as PDF" for its portions still in the freezer, to print more than one batch on a sheet, or reprint.

3 · In the freezer, and taking one out

Portions are ice-blue chips on the coffee's page. Tap one: Take it out (it becomes the bag Brew uses next) or Gave it away (one tap; it leaves your freezer, and its code page keeps working for your friend). Or scan its QR when you're signed in: the same two buttons are at the top of its page.
